Legal & Patient Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Sri Sai Skin & Hair Clinic, Hosapete, Karnataka

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an appointment request on our website or register at our reception desk, we collect basic personal and clinical details including your full name, mobile number, age, city or locality, gender, body weight measured at the clinic, and consultation records.

2. How We Use Patient Information

Your information is used strictly for scheduling clinical appointments, maintaining accurate dermatological records, generating medical prescriptions, and communicating appointment timings. We do not sell, rent, or trade patient data to third-party marketers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Patient intake and consultation records are stored on encrypted cloud infrastructure with role-based authentication restricted exclusively to authorized clinic personnel and consulting physicians.

4. Patient Rights & Contact

Under applicable Indian data protection standards, you may request access to or correction of your clinical records by visiting Sri Sai Skin & Hair Clinic, Station Road, Near Puneeth Rajkumar Circle, Patil Nagar, Hosapete, Karnataka - 583201, or by calling 9988443959.
